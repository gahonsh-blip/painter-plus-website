How the Studio works

🎨

Choose a scene

Living room, bedroom, kitchen, bathroom, dining, office, kids' room or exterior — each is a full demo built so nothing breaks when you switch.

🖌️

Pick a surface

Select which wall or element to paint, then choose a colour. The room updates live as paint fills the wall.

✨

Try finishes

Matte, eggshell, satin or semi-gloss — see how the sheen changes the look under different light.

📐

Measure & estimate

Type each surface's length and height to see its square feet, the total, and the litres of paint you'll need.

💾

Save & share

Save your scheme, copy a shareable link, and send it with your quote request. We match it on site.
